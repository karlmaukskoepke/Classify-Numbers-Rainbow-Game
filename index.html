How to Play

  1. A number appears at the top with its decimal expansion shown as color-coded squares — each digit has a unique color.
  2. Drag it down toward Rational or Irrational. Hovering either side reveals the subcategories.
  3. Drop it in the correct zone. Perfect placements (right side, first try) earn bonus 💎 points.
  4. You have 3 lives ❤️❤️❤️. Each wrong answer loses a life and resets your score to zero.
